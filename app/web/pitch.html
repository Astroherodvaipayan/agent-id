    04 / Under the hood

    How a proposal
    moves safely.

    Each creator has a separate negotiation lane. The operator approves the exact proposal before any dispatch begins.

    1. 01

      Brief & creator lanes

      Campaign Studio turns the brief into creator-specific threads and tracks the shared campaign budget.

    2. 02

      Human approval

      The operator reviews the recipient, wording, amount and scope. The backend freezes an exact proposal and approval record.

    3. 03

      Coordinator & workers

      The coordinator runs up to three creator workers concurrently. Each opens an AgntID task and delegates only the approved proposal tool.

    4. 04
      AgntID Runtime · control point

      Task, delegation & tool check

      The worker sends the task and delegation to AgntID Runtime. The runtime checks the visible tool and policy before the wrapped MCP call can proceed. A denial stops that lane.

    5. 05

      MCP tool & outbox

      The approved call reaches send_creator_proposal. The backend checks approval again, reserves the amount in its atomic shared-budget ledger, and dispatches the nonbinding email.

    6. 06

      Reply & review

      Replies enter the creator lane. Suggested negotiation changes return to the operator for review and a fresh approval before another proposal.

    The shared-budget ledger is enforced by Campaign Studio; the per-call policy belongs to AgntID. Live Runtime policy enforcement and email delivery are still pending verification.
